# Planetary Textures Licensing & Provenance Documentation

All surface maps and planetary ring textures in `public/assets/planets/` are derived from scientific observations collected by NASA planetary exploration missions (MESSENGER, Magellan, Clementine, Viking, Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, Cassini-Huygens, Voyager 1 & 2) and processed by Solar System Scope.

## Asset Directory Structure
- `mercury/mercury.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA MESSENGER surface mosaic)
- `venus/venus.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Magellan Radar & Pioneer Venus Ultraviolet cloud deck)
- `earth/earth_albedo.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Visible Earth / Blue Marble Next Generation)
- `earth/earth_bump.jpg` (NASA SRTM Topography Elevation)
- `earth/earth_clouds.png` (NASA Terra / Aqua MODIS Composite)
- `earth/earth_landmask.png` (USGS Land/Sea Binary Mask)
- `earth/earth_nightlights.jpg` (NASA Suomi NPP VIIRS City Lights)
- `mars/mars.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Viking & MGS MOC color mosaic)
- `jupiter/jupiter.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Cassini & Hubble Space Telescope Outer Planet Atmospheres Legacy)
- `saturn/saturn.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Cassini ISS Wide-Angle Camera)
- `saturn/saturn_ring.png` (NASA Cassini Ring UV/Visual Transmission Opacity Profile)
- `uranus/uranus.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Voyager 2 ISS Narrow-Angle Camera)
- `neptune/neptune.jpg` (2048x1024, NASA Voyager 2 ISS Color Composite)
